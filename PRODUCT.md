# ALUON Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Homeowners, architects, builders, and property professionals evaluating aluminum doors, gates, and enclosure solutions. They need to understand quality quickly, compare visual directions, and move from inspiration to a credible consultation without friction.

## Product Purpose

ALUON presents aluminum systems as considered architectural products rather than commodity catalog items. The website should create confidence, make product discovery visual, and turn a clear project intention into a useful consultation request.

## Brand Personality

Precise, architectural, assured. The experience should feel premium, calm, tactile, and expert without becoming cold or ornamental.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated landing pages with oversized slogans and interchangeable cards.
- Competitor cloning: do not reproduce Marver or Portalum branding, copy, layouts, or assets.
- Discount-led ecommerce patterns that make architectural products feel disposable.
- Dense catalog grids without context, hierarchy, or a clear next step.

## Design Principles

1. Show the material and the result before explaining the system.
2. Turn comparison into guided discovery, not an inventory dump.
3. Make expertise visible through restraint, detail, and useful context.
4. Every section must lead naturally to a next action or a better decision.
5. Premium means composed and dependable, not merely large type and dark imagery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-complete flows, visible focus, readable contrast, descriptive image alternatives, and reduced-motion support. Never let consent UI obscure essential content or actions.

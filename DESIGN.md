# ALUON Design Direction

## Visual Theme

Warm architectural editorial: mineral neutrals, deep ink, restrained oxblood, and generous daylight. Use real product imagery with quiet overlays instead of generic gradients.

## Color Roles

- Paper: warm off-white for the primary canvas.
- Ink: softened near-black for headings and navigation.
- Oxblood: focused accent for primary actions and active states.
- Clay: secondary highlight for editorial emphasis.
- Line: low-contrast warm divider.

## Typography

Use a strong display face for short architectural headlines and a highly legible sans-serif for body copy, labels, and controls. Keep long text within a readable measure and preserve a clear scale contrast between display, section, body, and metadata.

## Composition

Prefer editorial asymmetry, strong image crops, intentional whitespace, and clear content bands. Use cards only where comparison is the task. Avoid wrapping every section in a rounded container.

## Interaction

Use quiet hover states, short purposeful transitions, strong focus rings, clear step/progress cues, and responsive layouts that preserve hierarchy rather than merely stacking desktop content.

## Quality Bar

The result should feel authored by an architectural brand team: distinctive, calm, useful, and precise. A visitor should understand what ALUON makes, why it is credible, and what to do next without reading every paragraph.
